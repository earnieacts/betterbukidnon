About the Office

What the General Services Officer does under the law

The Provincial General Services Office (PGSO) looks after the property and support services of the Provincial Government of Bukidnon. Under the Local Government Code of 1991 (Republic Act No. 7160), the General Services Officer takes custody of and manages provincial property, supervises the use of public buildings and equipment, and recommends their proper upkeep and repair.

The PGSO mainly serves provincial offices. Residents deal with it less often than with offices such as the Treasurer or the PSWDO. Services like public markets and tricycle franchising belong to your city or municipality.

How to Reach the Office

General steps. See the Citizen's Charter for official steps.

Steps

  • Call the office or visit it at the Capitol and say what you need.
  • Ask whether your request is handled by the PGSO or by another provincial office.
  • Submit a written request if the office asks for one.
  • Keep a copy of your request and the name of the person who received it.

What to Bring

  • A valid government-issued ID
  • A written letter stating what you need and for what purpose
  • For an organization, a letter from its officers and the representative's ID
  • Requirements may vary; confirm with the office.

Handled by Your City or Municipality

These services are not provided by the Province

Public Markets and Slaughterhouses

Run by the city or municipality. Ask your city or municipal hall.

Tricycle Franchising

Handled by the city or municipality where the tricycle operates.

City and Municipal Facilities

Halls, gymnasiums and other facilities owned by a city or municipality are requested from that local government.

Tips

  • Call ahead to confirm whether the PGSO is the right office.
  • Put requests in writing and keep a copy.
  • Deal only with the office itself, and ask for a receipt for any payment.
